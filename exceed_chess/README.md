# Exceed Chess

> Chess that evolves. Captures fuel upgrades. At Turn 50, the board fractures.

---

## What is Exceed Chess?

Standard chess — until it isn't. Every capture earns **Soul Points (SP)**
you spend on upgrades and super-units. At Turn 50 the board fractures into
four islands, and expands every 50 turns after that.

Built in **Godot 4 / GDScript**. Releasing on itch.io first, then Google
Play and Steam.
